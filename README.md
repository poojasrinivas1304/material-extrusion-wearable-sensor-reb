# Material-extrusion wearable sensor reliability evidence audit

This repository package accompanies the review manuscript, “Process–structure–property determinants of reliability in material-extrusion wearable sensors: a systematic review and evidence audit.”

It contains the final consensus Reliability Evidence Benchmark (REB) matrix and the reviewer-agreement and adjudication record supporting the quantitative audit. Full-text articles reviewed in the study are not redistributed.

## Contents

- `Supplementary_Data_1_REB_Coding_Agreement_and_Adjudication.xlsx`: reviewer-specific ratings, agreement calculations, adjudication record, evidence locations, coding rules, and final consensus matrix.
- `REB_primary_audit_matrix.csv`: machine-readable final consensus matrix.

## Agreement analysis

Two reviewers independently applied the predefined L1–L8 coding rules to 29 studies. Agreement was evaluated on the 232 pre-consensus binary decisions. Exact agreement was 96.98%, overall Cohen's kappa was 0.921, PABAK was 0.940, and Gwet's AC1 was 0.951. Seven disagreements were resolved jointly using the predefined rule and located source evidence. Consensus decisions were used for the final audit; they were not used to recalculate agreement.

## Final audit values

- L1–L8 positive counts: 19, 29, 28, 29, 24, 18, 25, and 4.
- Mean R: 0.759.
- Range of R: 0.500–1.000.
- Studies reporting zero to four reliability-validation dimensions: 1, 2, 12, 11, and 3.

No full-paper PDFs are included in this package.
