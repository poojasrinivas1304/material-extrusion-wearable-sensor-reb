# Reliability-driven benchmarking reveals translational gaps in additively manufactured wearable sensors

This repository is the public data package for the 76-study systematic review by Mohamed Elgendi, Pooja Gurram, Swarubini P. J., Alexander V. Shokurov, and Carlo Menon.

The review included 76 studies and 80 eligible FDM/FFF sensor configurations. Eligible studies reported original experimental sensor-response data from a wearable or clearly wearable-relevant device. FDM/FFF had to fabricate the sensing element or a structure essential to transduction or mechanical load transfer.

## Reliability Evidence Benchmark

The eight-component Reliability Evidence Benchmark (REB) records whether predefined evidence was explicitly reported. It is not a pass/fail score or a measure of intrinsic device reliability.

| Item | Evidence dimension | Studies meeting the criterion |
|---|---|---:|
| L1 | Fabrication parameters | 33/76 (43.4%) |
| L2 | Material composition | 75/76 (98.7%) |
| L3 | Calibration or reference method | 76/76 (100.0%) |
| L4 | Quantitative response metric | 76/76 (100.0%) |
| L5 | Repeated-measurement stability | 62/76 (81.6%) |
| L6 | Environmental or use-related exposure | 31/76 (40.8%) |
| L7 | Integration or interface integrity | 29/76 (38.2%) |
| L8 | Independent-device variation | 17/76 (22.4%) |

The mean descriptive REB score was 0.656, the median was 0.625, and the range was 0.375–1.000. For L5–L8, 6 studies met no dimensions, 24 met one, 24 met two, 21 met three, and 1 met all four.

Final-round reviewer agreement after targeted reassessment was 99.8% (605/606; Cohen's κ = 0.996). Two unresolved Reviewer 2 L7 entries were excluded from this calculation. The final consensus matrix was used for synthesis.

## Reproducibility package

1. [**Supplementary Data 1**](Supplementary_Data_1_Master_Extraction_76_Studies.xlsx) — study extraction database and final study-level REB profiles.
2. [**Supplementary Data 2**](Supplementary_Data_2_Final_Full_Text_Eligibility_76.xlsx) — report-level full-text eligibility decisions.
3. [**Supplementary Data 3**](Supplementary_Data_3_Executed_Search_Strategies_2026-09-01.docx) — executed database-specific search strategies.
4. [**Supplementary Data 4**](Supplementary_Data_4_Final_Consensus_and_Statistical_Analysis_76.xlsx) — reviewer ratings, final-round agreement calculations, evidence log, adjudication record, and final consensus matrices.

The author-developed assessment includes seven methodological-concern domains (D1–D7) and one wearable-applicability domain (D8). It is distinct from the REB.

Publisher-provided full-text articles and supplementary files are not redistributed.
