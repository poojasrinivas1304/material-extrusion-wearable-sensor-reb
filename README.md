# Material-extrusion wearable sensor reliability evidence audit

This repository package accompanies the review manuscript, “Process–structure–property determinants of reliability in material-extrusion wearable sensors: a systematic review and evidence audit.”

It contains the manuscript source, final supplementary files, the consensus Reliability Evidence Benchmark (REB) matrix, and the reviewer-agreement and adjudication record. Full-text articles reviewed in the study are not redistributed.

## Contents

- `manuscript/`: LaTeX source, bibliography, class/style files, and manuscript figures.
- `figures/`: recalculated supplementary audit graphics.
- `supplementary/`: final Supplementary Information in Word and PDF formats.
- `data/Supplementary_Data_1_REB_Coding_Agreement_and_Adjudication.xlsx`: reviewer-specific ratings, formula-based agreement calculations, adjudication record, evidence locations, coding rules, and final consensus matrix.
- `data/REB_primary_audit_matrix.csv`: machine-readable final consensus matrix.

## Agreement analysis

Two reviewers independently applied the predefined L1–L8 coding rules to 29 studies. Agreement was evaluated on the 232 pre-consensus binary decisions. Exact agreement was 96.98% and overall Cohen's kappa was 0.921. Seven disagreements were resolved jointly using the predefined rule and located source evidence. Consensus decisions were used for the final audit; they were not used to recalculate agreement.

## Final audit values

- L1–L8 positive counts: 19, 29, 28, 29, 24, 18, 25, and 4.
- Mean R: 0.759.
- Range of R: 0.500–1.000.
- Studies reporting zero to four reliability-validation dimensions: 1, 2, 12, 11, and 3.

No full-paper PDFs are included in this package.
